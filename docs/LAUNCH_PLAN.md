## Executive Summary

Ash Strategy is a **tokenized Pokemon card investment fund** on Avalanche. $CATCH tokens represent fractional ownership in a curated portfolio of museum-quality graded cards (PSA/BGS 9-10).

**Why now:**
- Pokemon TCG market is $15B+ annually (growing 15% YoY)
- High-grade vintage cards have outperformed S&P 500 over 10 years
- Crypto-native collectors want liquid exposure without custody headaches
- No credible on-chain Pokemon card fund exists yet

**Raise:** 65,000 AVAX (~$1.6M at $25/AVAX) across 3 rounds  
**Target Portfolio:** 45+ museum-quality graded cards  
**Network:** Avalanche C-Chain

## 9. Legal & Compliance

### Structure Options

**Option A: BVI Investment Fund**
- Pros: Crypto-friendly, low tax, established framework
- Cons: Complexity, ongoing compliance costs
- Cost: $15-25K setup, $5K/year maintenance

**Option B: Swiss Association (Verein)**
- Pros: DAO-aligned, member-based, EU-adjacent
- Cons: Less precedent for investment funds
- Cost: $10-15K setup, $3K/year

**Option C: Cayman Foundation Company**
- Pros: Gold standard for crypto funds, clear governance
- Cons: Expensive, complex
- Cost: $30-50K setup, $15K/year

**Recommendation:** Start with Option B (Swiss) for cost efficiency, upgrade to Cayman if AUM exceeds $5M.

### Securities Considerations

- **Not a security (argument):** $CATCH is a utility token for governance; no promise of profits from managerial efforts
- **Mitigations:** 
  - Geo-block US IPs on dApp
  - No "investment" language in marketing
  - Governance = utility, not passive income
  - Consult securities lawyer before Round 1

### Tax

- Fund entity: 0% in BVI/Cayman, low in Switzerland
- Token holders: Responsible for own jurisdiction
- NFT/card acquisitions: VAT considerations in EU

## Appendix B: Competitor Deep Dive

### Rally Rd (Shutdown 2023)

- **Model:** Fractional equity in collectibles (cars, cards, art)
- **Failure reasons:** Regulatory pressure (SEC), illiquid secondary market, high fees
- **Lesson:** On-chain liquidity + non-US structure avoids their pitfalls

### Courtyard

- **Model:** NFT-backed physical cards (1:1)
- **Pros:** Real custody solution, growing user base
- **Cons:** No fund structure, single-card exposure only
- **Opportunity:** Partner with Courtyard for custody/verification?

### Alt

- **Model:** RWA platform for alternative assets
- **Pros:** Broad category coverage, institutional backing
- **Cons:** Not crypto-native, high minimums, not Pokemon-focused
- **Lesson:** Niche focus (Pokemon only) beats broad platform
